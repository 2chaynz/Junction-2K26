# Stoa: Make Blueprints Talk

Source: https://hackjunction.app/participate/45/challenges/335 (login needed)

## Task
- Program that answers natural-language questions about construction drawings and BIM.
- Answers must be correct and verifiable: show where in the drawing the answer came from, so a human can check in seconds.
- General-purpose AI fails on complex technical drawings. Site engineers lose hours a day finding info.
- Wanted: solutions future frontier models won't obsolete. Hybrids: frontier model + computer vision, 3D, geometry, small fine-tuned models.

## Data
- Real drawings and models, many formats and languages, with example questions.
- Preview (0.5 GB): https://fwjydgxpttajiqpfmqtj.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/stoa-junction-2026/junction-dataset-preview.zip
- Full (10 GB): https://fwjydgxpttajiqpfmqtj.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/stoa-junction-2026/junction-dataset.zip

## Judging (25% each)
- Accuracy of answers
- Visualizing reasoning so a human can verify
- Creativity
- Low inference cost
