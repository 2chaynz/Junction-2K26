## What exists already
- **Frontier VLMs are bad at this:** AECV-Bench (arXiv 2601.04819) — best models 0.39–0.51 exact match counting doors/windows/toilets on plans. Our pitch: specialist beats frontier.
- **CubiCasa5K ships SVG labels** for ~5k plans with exactly our classes (window, door, toilet, sink, sauna bench, bathtub…) + pretrained weights (github.com/CubiCasa/CubiCasa5k).
- **IFC-Bench v2** (arXiv 2605.01698, HF `sylvainHellin/ifc-bench`): source of our `ifc_bench` Qs. Adaptive LLM exploration of the IFC beats one-shot query generation.
- HF community models, all unproven (no evals): `rimashussain/gemma4-cubicasa-floorplan` (Gemma 4 E4B LoRA), `karanjaWakaba/Yolo_detection_cubicasa`, `mudasir13cs/floorcad-yolov8n-detect` (AGPL), `minemaster01/qwen25-vl-3b-floorplan-sft`. Worth a 10-min baseline each.
- Exemplar counting: CountGD (arXiv 2407.04619), CountZES (2512.16415) — "click one, find all".
- ⚠ **Contamination:** preview `colorful-NNNNN` images are likely in public CubiCasa5K. Hold them out of any training, or self-scores are inflated. Ask Stoa what the hidden test set is.

## Idea 1 (backbone): Format router with "receipts"
- IFC → ifcopenshell; vector PDF/DXF → text + polygon lookup; raster → detector. Gemma only parses the question and phrases the answer.
- Every answer returns a highlight (bbox/polygon) + cost. Most vector/BIM answers cost €0 and are exact.
- **Buildable:** yes; IFC path works today.

## Idea 2: Purpose-built detector for raster plans (the 53%)
- Train YOLO11 / RT-DETR on CubiCasa5K labels (preview held out) + OCR for room labels (MH, KH, SAUNA). A few GPU hours.
- Detections are the visual proof. Near-zero inference cost.
- **Test:** windows/WC/door counts on preview vs answer key.

## Idea 3 (the pitch): RL-trained Gemma 4 that uses tools
- Answers are auto-gradable (exact count / 2% area) → ~120k verifiable rewards. Train Gemma 4 E4B with GRPO to call `detect`, `read_text`, `ifc_query`, `measure_polygon`.
- Output = small, cheap model whose reasoning is a visible tool trace tied to highlights. Hits all 4 criteria + Gemma track + "not obsoleted by next year's models".
- **Risk:** RL is fiddly in 36h. Fallback: SFT on tool traces generated by Ideas 1–2.

## Idea 4: Synthetic data engine
- Swiss/IFC geometry is exact → render to raster with scan noise, rotation, other languages/styles = unlimited perfectly labelled data for Idea 2/3, and a generalisation story beyond CubiCasa.

## Idea 5 (weird): Drawings that check themselves
- Detector counts, room labels, schedules and IFC should agree. Flag mismatches ("schedule: 12 doors, plan: 11") with both highlights. Doubles as free confidence (translated twin Qs + multi-format drawings) and links to Stoa's "2D change quantities" gap.
