# T? Highlight on scans: OCR the marked spot before the VLM

- **Goal:** highlight a room on a scan, ask "what is this?" / "area of this room?" → answer from OCR at €0, label circled. VLM only when nothing readable.
- **How:** `ocr.read_crop(img)`: OCR at 0/90/180/270°, small crops upscaled, best reading per spot (lexicon/area match first, then confidence). Many CubiCasa sheets are rotated 90°: on colorful-2002 whole-sheet OCR misses OH, PH, ET, K; rotated crops read them at 0.9+. Lexicon + living (OH), hall (ET), closet (VH); mirrored labels ("HO") matched reversed.
- **Files:** `sitelens/region.py`, `sitelens/ocr.py` (new `read_crop`, lexicon only; `words`/`labels` unchanged), `tests/test_region.py`.
