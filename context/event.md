# Event overview

Source: team one-pager (Junction_2026_guide.pdf) + Participant Guidebook. Schedule is preliminary; check Junction Platform and Discord.

## Basics
- Fri 13 – Sun 15 Nov 2026, 48h. Hype Areena, Vanha Jorvaksentie 3, Espoo.
- Theme: "European Tech Renaissance". Not AI-only, but most challenges are AI.
- Teams 1–4. Submissions open-sourced under MIT.
- Platforms: Junction Platform (hackjunction.app: team, submission, review), Discord (announcements, mentors in challenge channels).

## Tracks and challenges
- Tracks: Hardware (Arduino), AI Infrastructure (Verda), Healthcare, FinTech, Agentic AI, Data.
- Named challenge: Cala "Make Unicorns Real": build a product for founders or VCs using public data. Cala = public data layer for AI agents: companies, people, investors, funding rounds and connections between them.
- Challenge partners: Hostinger, Valio Aimo, S-Pankki, Aalto, Cala, City of Espoo, Exa, MariaDB, AfterQuery, Bahn Express, AstraZeneca, Stoa, Intel, Context.dev, Rokoko, Ignite Next, Meta, Tieto. Briefs revealed at opening.
- Tech partners (credits, some prizes for best use): OpenAI Codex, NVIDIA, Anthropic, Featherless, Modal, Qualia, Cloudinary, Aiven, Oxylabs, Wendy, Scailab, IronShard, CRACI, Atech.dev.
- Hardware Lab (Arduino, 5F): UNO Q boards, Modulino sensors, 3D printers, LeRobot arms, mentors. MLH lab: Arduinos, Raspberry Pis.

## Judging flow
1. Each partner judges its own challenge with its own criteria.
2. Teams peer-review face to face to pick the top 5.
3. Top 5 pitch live (3 min + 3 min Q&A) to a 7-judge jury: Supercell, Lifeline Ventures, Google DeepMind, Verda, AfterQuery, Context.dev, Founders House.
