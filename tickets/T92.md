# T? Pages never served stale after a rebuild

- **Goal:** index.html had no Cache-Control, so a browser could reuse an old page (and old bundle) after a rebuild: "I do not see the changes".
- **How:** middleware: HTML pages `no-cache` (always revalidate), hashed `/assets/` immutable for a year, `/api/` untouched.
- **Files:** `sitelens/server.py`.
