# T? Measure: area + length takeoffs with scale and references (Bluebeam-style)

- **Goal:** auto takeoff (room polygons, areas, perimeters, wall lengths) with the scale shown and a plain-language summary; manual takeoffs (calibrate, length, area, count) drawn on the sheet.
- **Scale:** DXF geometry, scale notes on the sheet (nearest viewport), scale bars on scans (OCR), same-stem siblings (PNG ↔ PDF), area labels. None found → ask, with options from other sheets of the same project (scale notes, known dimensions). Every scale keeps its references (file, page, box, text), stored and shown.
- **Store:** `.sitelens/measure/<hash>.json` per drawing (gitignored, `SITELENS_STORE`).
- **Files:** `sitelens/measure.py`, `sitelens/server.py` (`/api/measure*`), `web/src/Measure.tsx`, `web/src/Viewer.tsx` (draw tools + shapes), `web/src/App.tsx` (toggle), `web/src/api.ts`, `web/src/types.ts`, `web/src/styles.css`, `tests/test_measure.py`, `CONTRACT.md`, `.gitignore`.
- **Needs:** none. Not T32's files (`takeoff.py`, `Takeoff.tsx`).
- **Done when:** Swiss plan shows rooms + scale "1:200, DXF agrees"; a HABS scan reads its scale bar or asks; cubicasa asks and calibrates from a drawn line; references listed and highlighted.
- **Verify:** `python tests/test_measure.py`; open Measure on a drawing.
