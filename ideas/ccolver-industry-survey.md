# Industry survey: would you use it?

For site/design engineers, estimators, foremen, BIM coordinators. ~5 min. Paste into Google Forms / Typeform.
Types: (single) one choice · (multi) several · (scale) 1–5 · (text) free text.

## Intro (form description)
> I'm working on a project with Stoa, a construction-tech startup. The tool lets you ask a question about a drawing or BIM model ("how many doors on level 2?", "net area of room 119?", "what changed between rev A and B?") and get the answer with the exact spot highlighted on the sheet. I'd like honest feedback: would you use it, and which features matter or are redundant? Answers are anonymous unless you leave contact details.

## 1. About you
1. Your role? (single) Site engineer · Foreman / site manager · Estimator / QS · Architect / designer · Structural / MEP engineer · BIM coordinator · Project manager · Other
2. Company type? (single) Main contractor · Subcontractor · Design office · Developer / client · Supplier · Other
3. How many hours a week do you spend looking things up in drawings, models or specs? (single) <1 · 1–3 · 3–8 · 8+
4. What files do you work with most? (multi) Vector PDF · Scanned PDF · DWG/DXF · IFC / BIM model · Schedules / spreadsheets · Specs (Word/PDF)

## 2. The problem today
5. What question do you most often need answered from drawings? (text)
6. Where do you lose the most time? (multi) Finding the right sheet · Reading / interpreting it · Cross-checking sheets · Tracking revisions · Quantities / takeoff · Waiting for the designer to reply
7. What do you use today? (multi) PDF viewer search · BIM viewer · Takeoff software (Bluebeam, etc.) · Ask a colleague / designer · Manual measuring · Other
8. Last time a drawing was misread on your project, what did it cost (time, rework, money)? (text, optional)

## 3. Would you use it?
9. How likely are you to use a tool like this weekly? (scale 1 = never, 5 = every week)
10. Main reason for your answer above? (text)
11. What would stop you using it? (multi) Don't trust AI answers · Too slow · Data/privacy rules · Doesn't fit our workflow / software · Our drawings are too messy · Cost · Nothing, I'd use it · Other

## 4. Features: useful or redundant?
12. Rate each feature (grid: Essential · Nice to have · Redundant / wouldn't use)
    - a. Ask a question in plain language (any language)
    - b. Answer with the exact spot highlighted on the drawing
    - c. Step-by-step "how we got this number"
    - d. Counts (doors, windows, fixtures)
    - e. Areas, lengths, volumes
    - f. Read schedules / tables (door, window, finish schedules)
    - g. What changed between two revisions, with quantity changes
    - h. Search across all sheets ("where is the fire door schedule?")
    - i. One-paragraph summary of a sheet
    - j. Confidence score / "please check this" flag
    - k. Cost per answer shown
    - l. Works on IFC / BIM models, not just PDFs
    - m. Voice / hands-free on site
    - n. Works offline on site
13. If you could keep only one feature, which? (text)
14. Anything missing that you'd actually pay for? (text)
